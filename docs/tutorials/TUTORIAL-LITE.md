# KHEL-O Tutorials: LITE mode (use this one first)

The full system in `TUTORIAL-VIDEO-SOP.md` is the "later, when we have time" version. For now: **fast, simple, good enough**. About 20–30 minutes of work per video, not half a day.

## The rules

1. **Plain phone screen recording. No fancy canvas, no frame, no zoom animations, no arrows.** Only the phone's own screen recorder, trimmed.
2. **One language recorded first.** Owners → Hindi first. Customers → English first. The second language comes later, only for videos people actually watch.
3. **Voice: you may record your own voice** on the phone while recording (Hindi, casual, like explaining to a friend). ElevenLabs is optional. Use it only if the recorded voice sounds bad.
4. **Captions: CapCut "Auto captions", fix the typos, done.** No styling work. Use ONE preset and never change it.
5. **15–40 seconds. One task per video.**
6. **Only 8 videos this week.** Everything else becomes a screenshot guide (see below).

## The 7 steps to make one video (per video)

1. **Rehearse once** on the demo account (never real customers/cafés). Know the exact taps.
2. **Write 5–8 short lines** of what you'll say. Keep button names in English. Hindi in simple Hinglish.
3. **Turn on Do Not Disturb**, then record the phone screen (Android/iPhone built-in screen recorder) with the mic on and say the lines **while** doing the taps. If you mess up, stop, wait 2 seconds, redo that line, and cut it later.
4. **CapCut:** import, trim the start/end and mistakes. That's all.
5. **Captions → Auto captions.** Fix wrong words (KHEL-O, Razorpay, Hindi words). Apply the same style every time.
6. **Add the same 2-second end card** (KHEL-O logo + "Need help? WhatsApp us"), made once and reused.
7. **Export 1080p, 30 fps.** Name it like `O03_prices_hi.mp4`. Watch it on a phone. Upload to the shared folder and post in the group.

If a video takes longer than an hour, ship it as it is and move on.

## No-video alternative for everything else: screenshot guides

For each smaller task, make a **5–6 slide image guide** (screenshots with one sentence each). Share it as a PDF or images on WhatsApp. 10 minutes each, no editing, no voice. Use Canva or Google Slides. Good for: offers, reviews, staff, notifications, cancellations, settings.

## What is still worth doing (small, high value)

- Check the video with the **sound off**. If the captions alone make sense, it's good.
