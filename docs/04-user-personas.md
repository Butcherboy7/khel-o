# User Personas — KHEL-O

## Overview

These personas represent the four primary user archetypes for the KHEL-O platform. They are based on market research and direct conversations with gaming café owners and gamers in Indian cities. Every product decision should be tested against at least one of these personas.

---

## Persona 1: Rajesh Kumar — Small Independent Café Owner

### Demographics

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Name | Rajesh Kumar |
| Age | 32 |
| City | Pune (Kothrud) |
| Role | Owner and sole operator of "GG Zone Gaming Café" |
| Education | B.Com from Pune University |
| Tech Comfort | Moderate. Uses WhatsApp, Instagram for business. Can navigate basic apps. |
| Language | Hindi, Marathi, basic English |

### Background

Rajesh opened GG Zone 18 months ago after saving for 3 years and taking a ₹12 lakh loan. He has 15 gaming PCs (mix of GTX 1650 and RTX 3060 builds), 2 PS5 consoles, and a small café area with snacks and cold drinks. His café is near Pune University, targeting college students.

He runs the café himself with one part-time helper. He opens at 10 AM and closes at midnight. He handles everything — from fixing PC issues to serving chai to managing his Instagram page where he posts gaming memes to attract customers.

### Current Behavior

- Gets most customers between 5 PM and 11 PM on weekdays and all day on weekends.
- From 10 AM to 3 PM on weekdays, only 2–4 of his 15 PCs are occupied. Sometimes zero.
- Advertises on Instagram and a local WhatsApp group for his café.
- Takes bookings over WhatsApp. No formal system. Sometimes double-books by mistake.
- Runs occasional "happy hour" discounts verbally — tells whoever walks in.
- Has no way to track how many customers come due to promotions vs walk-ins.
- Monthly revenue: approximately ₹1.5–2 lakhs. Barely covering costs after loan EMI.

### Goals

1. **Fill empty PCs between 10 AM and 4 PM on weekdays.** This is his biggest problem. Even 3–4 extra bookings per day during this window would be significant.
2. **Reach new customers** who don't already know about GG Zone. His Instagram reach is limited.
3. **Reduce WhatsApp booking chaos.** He wants a simple system where bookings come in and he just manages them.
4. **Understand which promotions work.** He has no data. He runs "50% off" sometimes but doesn't know if it brought anyone new.
5. **Grow revenue by 30–40%** within 6 months of using the platform.

### Frustrations

1. "I spent ₹15 lakh on equipment and it sits idle half the day. That's money sleeping."
2. "WhatsApp booking is a mess. I forgot to reply to a customer last week and he went to the café across the street."
3. "I post on Instagram every day but only get 50 likes. I need to reach the 5,000 students within 2 km of my café."
4. "I tried Google My Business but gamers don't search for cafés on Google. They ask friends."
5. "I don't know if my ₹100/hour pricing is competitive. I have no idea what others charge."

### How KHEL-O Helps Rajesh

- **Discovery:** His café appears on KHEL-O with full details, photos, and hardware specs. Gamers within 5 km can find him.
- **Promotions:** He creates a "Weekday Afternoon Special: RTX 3060 tier, 11 AM–3 PM, 30% off" promotion. KHEL-O pushes this to nearby gamers.
- **Booking Management:** Bookings come through the app. No more WhatsApp chaos. He sees a clean list of today's bookings.
- **Analytics:** He can see how many bookings each promotion generated. He learns that 2 PM–4 PM with 25% off fills 4 extra seats.
- **Free Dashboard:** He doesn't pay to list his café. KHEL-O earns from gamer convenience fees.

### Key Quote

> "I don't need fancy software. I need more butts in seats between 11 and 4."

## Persona 2: Priya Mehta — Multi-Location Café Owner

### Demographics

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Name | Priya Mehta |
| Age | 28 |
| City | Bengaluru (operates in Koramangala, HSR Layout, and Whitefield) |
| Role | Co-founder and business lead of "FragHouse Gaming" (3 locations) |
| Education | MBA from Christ University, Bengaluru |
| Tech Comfort | High. Uses analytics tools, spreadsheets, and has a basic understanding of tech stacks. |
| Language | English, Hindi, Kannada |

### Background

Priya co-founded FragHouse Gaming with her brother (who handles the technical/hardware side) 2 years ago. They started with one location in Koramangala and expanded to 3 locations. Each location has 20–30 PCs, 4 PS5 consoles, and a proper café with food and beverages.

FragHouse is positioned as a premium gaming experience — air-conditioned, themed interiors, good peripherals, and reliable internet. They charge ₹150–250/hour depending on the tier. They have a dedicated Instagram following of 12,000.

Priya manages the business side — pricing, marketing, staff, and expansion plans. She currently tracks everything in Google Sheets and uses a shared WhatsApp group for each location to manage bookings.

### Current Behavior

- Each location has a manager who handles walk-ins and WhatsApp bookings.
- No unified view across locations. She calls each manager to get daily numbers.
- Runs seasonal promotions (exam season discounts, weekend tournaments) but promotion execution is inconsistent across locations.
- Koramangala location runs at 70% occupancy. HSR at 55%. Whitefield at 40% (newer location, still building awareness).
- She spends ₹30,000/month on Instagram and Google ads but cannot attribute bookings to specific campaigns.
- Considering a 4th location but unsure about demand data in potential areas.

### Goals

1. **Unified dashboard** across all 3 locations. See bookings, revenue, and occupancy in one place.
2. **Increase Whitefield occupancy** from 40% to 60%. New location needs customer acquisition.
3. **Data-driven promotion decisions.** She wants to know: "If I run 25% off at HSR from 12–3 PM on weekdays, how many additional bookings will it generate?"
4. **Reduce operational overhead.** Three WhatsApp groups for booking management is unsustainable.
5. **Standardize pricing and promotions** across locations while allowing location-specific adjustments.

### Frustrations

1. "I have no single source of truth. I'm running a ₹50 lakh business on WhatsApp and Google Sheets."
2. "Each location manager has a different style. One gives arbitrary discounts to friends. I can't standardize."
3. "I spend money on Instagram ads but I can't track how many bookings they generate."
4. "Whitefield is bleeding money. I need a way to generate awareness in that area specifically."
5. "I want to expand to Chennai but I have no demand data to justify the investment."

### How KHEL-O Helps Priya

- **Multi-Location View (Post-MVP):** Eventually, she can manage all 3 locations from one dashboard. In MVP, she creates separate accounts but sees per-location data.
- **Targeted Promotions:** She runs area-specific promotions. "HSR Layout, weekday afternoon, 20% off" reaches gamers in HSR. Whitefield gets a different promotion.
- **Attribution:** Every booking via KHEL-O is tracked. She knows exactly how many bookings each promotion generates.
- **Demand Insights (Post-MVP):** Heatmaps showing demand by hour and location help her make data-driven decisions.
- **Online Payments:** Standardized pricing. No more managers giving arbitrary discounts.

### Key Quote

> "I need data, not guesswork. Show me where the demand is and I'll put the PCs there."

## Persona 3: Arjun Patel — College Gamer (Budget-Conscious)

### Demographics

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Name | Arjun Patel |
| Age | 20 |
| City | Hyderabad (Gachibowli area, near IIIT-H) |
| Role | 3rd-year B.Tech student at IIIT Hyderabad |
| Monthly Budget for Gaming | ₹1,000–1,500 (from pocket money) |
| Tech Comfort | Very high. Grew up with tech. Comfortable with any app. |
| Language | English, Hindi, Telugu |
| Games | Valorant, CS2, GTA V, Fortnite |

### Background

Arjun is a computer science student who loves gaming but doesn't own a gaming PC. His college laptop (an i5 with integrated graphics) can barely run Valorant at low settings. He visits gaming cafés 3–4 times a week, usually after classes.

He has a group of 4 friends who often go to gaming cafés together. They look for the cheapest option that has decent hardware. They've tried 6 different cafés near campus over the past year and have settled on 2 regulars — not because they're the best, but because they're the most familiar.

Arjun tracks his gaming budget carefully. At ₹80–120/hour, each session costs him ₹200–360. He's always looking for discounts and happy hours.

### Current Behavior

- Goes to the same 2 cafés because he doesn't know about others in the area.
- Walks in without booking. Sometimes all PCs are occupied and he wastes 30 minutes waiting.
- Hears about discounts through word of mouth or café WhatsApp groups.
- Has no way to compare pricing across cafés in his area.
- Occasionally tries a new café but has been disappointed by misleading claims ("RTX 3060" turned out to be a 1650).
- Coordinates gaming sessions with friends over WhatsApp — deciding which café, what time, who's coming.

### Goals

1. **Find affordable gaming sessions.** He needs the best price for decent hardware.
2. **Discover promotions and discounts.** If a café near campus has 30% off between 2–5 PM, he wants to know.
3. **Guarantee a seat.** He doesn't want to walk 15 minutes to a café only to find it full.
4. **Know what hardware he's getting.** He wants to trust that "Premium Tier" actually means an RTX 3060 with a good monitor.
5. **Book sessions with friends.** Coordinating 4 people for the same time slot at the same café shouldn't require 50 WhatsApp messages.

### Frustrations

1. "I walked to CyberHub café after class. Full. Walked to NextGen. Also full. Ended up going back to my hostel. Wasted an hour."
2. "This café says 'high-end PCs' on their Google listing but the hardware is 5 years old."
3. "I heard about a happy hour at some café from a friend but it was over by the time I got there."
4. "I don't know which cafés near me have RTX 3060 or better. I have to physically go and check."
5. "₹120/hour is a lot for me. If there's a café offering ₹80/hour for the same specs, I want to know."

### How KHEL-O Helps Arjun

- **Discovery:** He searches "Gaming cafés near Gachibowli" and sees all options with hardware specs, pricing, and ratings. No more guessing.
- **Promotions:** He sees "TechArena Café — Premium Tier, 2 PM–5 PM, 30% off" on his home screen. That's ₹84/hour instead of ₹120. Exactly his free period.
- **Guaranteed Seat:** He books in advance. When he arrives, a PC in his tier is guaranteed.
- **Hardware Transparency:** Each café lists their hardware tiers with specific GPU, CPU, RAM, and monitor specs. No more bait-and-switch.
- **Price Comparison:** He can sort cafés by price for "Premium Tier" and find the best deal.
- **Reviews:** He reads reviews from other gamers before trying a new café.

### Key Quote

> "I just want to know where I can get an RTX 3060 for the cheapest rate near my college. That's it."

## Persona 4: Sneha Reddy — Competitive / Esports Gamer

### Demographics

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Name | Sneha Reddy |
| Age | 23 |
| City | Mumbai (Andheri East) |
| Role | Semi-professional Valorant player. Part-time content creator. Works at a startup. |
| Monthly Budget for Gaming | ₹3,000–5,000 (willing to pay for quality) |
| Tech Comfort | Expert. Builds PCs. Understands specs deeply. |
| Language | English, Hindi |
| Games | Valorant (primary, competitive), CS2, Apex Legends |

### Background

Sneha has been gaming competitively for 4 years. She was part of a college esports team that placed 2nd in an inter-college tournament. She now plays in online tournaments and scrims with her team (5 players, scattered across Mumbai).

She owns a decent gaming laptop (RTX 3060, 144Hz) but for competitive play and tournament practice, she prefers a desktop setup with a 240Hz monitor, mechanical keyboard, and wired mouse — things she can find at premium gaming cafés.

She visits gaming cafés 2–3 times a week, specifically for practice sessions with her team. For team practice, they need 5 PCs in the same tier, preferably sitting together.

She earns money from tournament winnings, content creation (YouTube and Instagram), and her day job. She's willing to pay ₹200–300/hour for a top-tier setup.

### Current Behavior

- Has a "main" café in Andheri that she trusts for hardware quality. She's been going there for 2 years.
- Books by calling the owner directly. He holds 5 PCs for her team on request.
- Has tried other cafés for team practice but been disappointed by inconsistent hardware, laggy internet, or uncomfortable chairs.
- Practices 3–4 evenings a week. Café sessions are usually 3–4 hours.
- Shares café reviews with the esports community. Her recommendation carries weight.
- Travels to other parts of Mumbai for tournaments at gaming lounges. Needs to find practice spaces near tournament venues.

### Goals

1. **Reliable high-end hardware.** She needs 240Hz, low input lag, RTX 3060 or better, wired peripherals. No compromises.
2. **Book for a team.** She needs to book 5 PCs in the same tier for the same time slot. This is a recurring need.
3. **Discover cafés in unfamiliar areas.** When a tournament is in Powai, she needs to find a practice café near Powai.
4. **Consistent experience.** She wants to know that "VIP Tier" at any KHEL-O café means a certain minimum standard.
5. **Schedule recurring sessions.** She practices every Tuesday and Thursday, 7–10 PM. She wants to book this in advance without repeating the process every week.

### Frustrations

1. "I booked 5 PCs at a new café for team practice. When we arrived, 2 of the 'RTX 3060' PCs were actually 1660 Supers. Unacceptable for competitive play."
2. "I can't find cafés when I travel to other parts of the city. Google Maps shows me internet cafés and print shops."
3. "Booking by calling the owner is unreliable. He forgot once and gave our PCs to walk-ins."
4. "I want a 240Hz monitor for competitive Valorant. Most cafés have 144Hz and claim they're 'high-end.' I need to know the exact specs."
5. "I wish I could book 5 PCs with one booking instead of making 5 separate bookings."

### How KHEL-O Helps Sneha

- **Hardware Transparency:** She filters cafés by GPU (RTX 3060+), monitor refresh rate (240Hz), and sees exact specs before booking.
- **Reliable Booking:** She books online. Confirmed. No more calling and hoping the owner remembers.
- **Location Discovery:** When she needs a practice space near a tournament venue in Powai, she searches on KHEL-O and finds options.
- **Quality Assurance:** Reviews from other competitive gamers help her evaluate new cafés. Hardware tier details provide transparency.
- **Team Booking (Future Enhancement):** In a future version, she could book multiple seats in one transaction. In MVP, she books individually but the tier availability system ensures all 5 PCs are available.

### Key Quote

> "I don't care about discounts. I care about hardware quality. Show me where the 240Hz monitors are and I'll pay full price."
