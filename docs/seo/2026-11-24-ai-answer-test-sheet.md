# AI answer test sheet

Run the same questions every month, same wording, logged out or in a fresh session, same city setting, and record the date and tool version. AI answers vary: one good result proves nothing, one miss proves nothing. Look at the trend over three or more runs.

## Questions (do not reword)

1. Where can I play GTA 6 in Hyderabad?
2. I don't have a PS5 but want to play GTA 6. What are my options?
3. Are there places to play GTA 6 in Hyderabad?
4. Can I rent a console to play GTA 6 in India?
5. What are the best ways to experience GTA 6 without buying a console?
6. Which gaming locations in Hyderabad offer GTA 6?
7. Where can I find a gaming cafe near me with GTA 6?

## Tools to test

ChatGPT (with search on), Google AI Overviews / AI Mode, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot.

## How to read the results

- Named but not cited: the AI knows the brand but did not use our pages. Improve the page that answers that question.
- Cited with wrong facts (price, location, "KHEL-O has cafés"): fix our page and `llms.txt`, and note the date. Never ask a tool to "remember" something.
- Not named at all: check the page is indexed (Search Console and Bing), then work on mentions on other sites (see the report).
- Competitor named: open their page and note what it has that ours lacks (a table, a price, reviews).
