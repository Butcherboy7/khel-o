# KHEL-O SEO, AEO and GEO plan (2026-11-20)

No search-volume or ranking tool is connected, so clusters below are grouped by intent, not by measured volume. Connect Search Console
(token env `NEXT_PUBLIC_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION`) and compare impressions per cluster after two weeks. A competitor (Gamers Guild) was said to
rank first on "pre-book"; I could not verify its wording, so this plan targets the phrase itself.

## What changed in this pass
- Home title "Pre-Book Gaming Cafés in Hyderabad: PS5, GTA 6, PC | KHEL-O" (59 chars) plus a pre-book description with a call to action.
- `/gta6` title "Pre-Book GTA 6 at a PS5 Café Near You | KHEL-O".
- Café pages: "<café> — Pre-Book (GTA 6) from ₹X/hr", description says "Plays GTA 6 on PS5" only for cafés that really have a PS5 / Xbox Series.
- Site default title, description and keywords carry pre-book / GTA 6 / PS5 terms.
- AEO/GEO: `robots.txt` has an explicit group for GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot,
  Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended. `llms.txt` states how pre-booking works and which consoles run GTA 6.
- Already in place and kept: sitemap (156 URLs), canonicals, Organization / WebSite / ItemList / VideoGame / LocalBusiness schema, FAQ and
  answer blocks on GTA 6 pages.

## Next, in order
1. Search Console: verify the domain, submit the sitemap, watch the "pre-book" and "GTA 6" queries.
2. Google Business Profile for KHEL-O, and ask partner cafés to link their profile to their khel-o.com café page.
3. Reviews on café pages are the strongest local and AI signal: ask each player after a booking.
4. Short answer pages in plain language for the questions AI assistants get ("how to pre-book GTA 6 at a gaming cafe", "is GTA 6 on PS4").
5. Reddit and local community mentions (r/hyderabad, r/IndianGaming) with honest answers, not ads. These weigh heavily in AI answers.
6. Re-run an AI visibility check monthly: ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude the 10 primary keywords above and record whether KHEL-O appears.
