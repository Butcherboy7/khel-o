## 1. Product Overview
- **One-liner:** Book gaming cafés and game zones near you: see live availability, compare prices, pay online, no calling ahead.
- **What it does:** Players find a gaming café (PC, PS5/PS4, VR, racing sims) or game zone (snooker, pool, bowling, arcade), pick a station and time, pay online (Razorpay, UPI), and get a QR pass the café scans at the desk. Café owners get a free listing, bookings, an owner dashboard with plain-language Insights, offers, staff accounts and weekly UPI payouts.
- **Category:** Two-sided local booking marketplace (players ↔ gaming cafés). Web app / PWA at khel-o.com, not in the app stores yet.
- **Pricing:**
  - *Players:* pay the café's price plus a small platform fee (about 4% on the final, discounted price).
  - *Cafés:* free to list, no setup fee, no monthly fee; KHEL-O takes its share from bookings and pays the rest weekly to the café's UPI ID.
  - TODO: confirm the exact current fee wording for public copy.
- **Features worth marketing:** live slot availability; co-op pricing (2+ players on one console, priced separately); offers that apply automatically at checkout; badges and XP (Day One OG badge, Early Voter); voting for cafés that aren't on KHEL-O yet ("Booking Soon").
- **Markets:** India. Live cafés in Hyderabad (e.g. DG Gaming Cafe, RockStar Gaming Cafe); "Booking Soon" lead listings in Bengaluru and elsewhere. TODO: current live-café count per city.

## 2. Target Audience
- **Primary (demand):** Gen Z and young millennial gamers aged about 16–28: college students and young working professionals in Indian metros (Hyderabad first). Many don't own a PS5, a gaming PC or a VR rig, or want to play with friends rather than alone.
- **Secondary (supply):** independent gaming café and game-zone owners. Small operators, often running bookings on calls, WhatsApp and walk-ins.
- **Jobs to be done (players):**
  - "Find a place to play with my squad tonight, and know there's a free station before we travel."
  - "Know what it'll cost before I go."
  - "Get a deal."
- **Jobs to be done (owners):**
  - "Fill empty stations on weekday afternoons."
  - "Get found by gamers who don't know my café exists."
  - "Stop handling bookings over the phone."

## 3. Personas
- **The Squad Planner (player):** organises the friend group's outing, compares cafés, cares about price per head and co-op deals. Shares links in the group chat. Biggest influence on group bookings.
- **The Solo Grinder (player):** plays regularly, wants a specific setup (high-Hz PC, PS5 Pro, racing rig), values availability and loyalty perks.
- **The Curious First-Timer (player):** saw a Reel, wants to try VR or a racing sim once. Price- and offer-sensitive; arrives through the Instagram in-app browser.
- **The Café Owner (supply):** decides alone, cash-flow sensitive, sceptical of platforms that take a cut. Persuaded by real demand (vote counts from "Booking Soon"), zero upfront cost and reliable weekly payouts.

## 4. Problems & Pain Points
- **Players:**
  - You have to call or DM a café to know whether a station is free, and often get no answer.
  - Prices are scattered across Instagram stories and posters.
  - Turning up to a full café wastes the trip.
  - It's hard to discover cafés beyond the one you already know.
- **Owners:**
  - Empty stations at off-peak times.
  - Discovery depends on word of mouth and the café's own Instagram.
  - Manual bookings, no-shows and no data on who comes back.
- **Emotional tension:** players want the night out to just work, without the awkward "is it free?" back-and-forth. Owners fear giving up margin to a platform that doesn't bring customers.

## 5. Competitive Landscape
- **Status quo (the real competitor):** calling the café, Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, walk-ins, Google Maps listings.
- **Adjacent booking apps:** sports and turf booking apps, and events/experiences apps. They cover sports turfs and events, not gaming-café stations by the slot. TODO: verify whether any of them have added gaming cafés in Hyderabad.
- **Café-side software:** café billing/POS and gaming-centre management tools run the café internally but don't bring new customers.
- **Gap KHEL-O fills:** a consumer marketplace built specifically for gaming cafés and game zones, with live station availability.

## 6. Differentiation
- Built only for gaming cafés and game zones; per-station, per-slot booking.
- Live availability plus online payment means no calling ahead.
- Co-op pricing that matches how squads actually play.
- Offers that apply automatically at checkout, so you never miss a deal.
- Free for cafés, with weekly UPI payouts.
- Demand-first expansion: players vote for cafés they want on KHEL-O, and the founders take those votes to the owner.

## 7. Objections & Anti-Personas
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "Why pay a platform fee when I can just call?" | You see live free stations and the final price before you leave home, and offers apply automatically. Often cheaper than the walk-in price. |
| "My café isn't on it." | Vote for it: we show owners the demand and bring them on. |
| (Owner) "You'll eat my margin." | Free to list, no monthly fee, paid weekly to your UPI. You only pay when we bring you a booking. |
| "Is the payment safe?" | Razorpay checkout and a QR pass you show at the desk. |

- **Anti-personas:**
  - Hardcore home-setup gamers who never leave the house.
  - Large chains that run their own booking systems.
  - Real-money gaming or betting audiences. KHEL-O is not a money-game platform; tournament prizes are fixed, never pooled from entry fees.

## 8. Switching Dynamics (JTBD Four Forces)
- **Push:** unanswered calls, turning up to a full café, not knowing the price.
- **Pull:** see what's free right now, deals, a booking in about 30 seconds, badges.
- **Habit:** "I always just go to my usual café and walk in."
- **Anxiety:**
  - Paying online to an unknown brand.
  - Signing in inside Instagram's in-app browser (72% of campaign visitors arrive there).
  - "Will the café honour it?"

## 9. Customer Language
- **Use:** gaming café, game zone, book a slot, station, setup, squad, co-op, 1P / 2P, PS5, VR, racing sim, snooker, pool, "no calling ahead", live availability, deal / offer.
- **Avoid:** "esports platform" (overclaims), "gambling / betting / real money", corporate words ("solutions", "leverage", "seamless").
- **Glossary:**
  - *Booking Soon* = a lead café that players can vote for.
  - *Day One / OG* = the early-supporter badge.
  - *Early Voter* = the badge for voting for a café.

## 10. Brand Voice
- **Tone:** playful, warm, grateful, Gen Z, in the style of Zepto and Swiggy. For example: "pls pls pls vote 🥺", "You're a legend 🫶", "Rally the squad 📣".
- **Style:** short sentences, plain words, emoji used for warmth (not a storm of them). Light Hinglish is fine on social. TODO: confirm how much Hinglish.
- **Founder-led:** founders Uzair and Bindu, a friends-built startup that runs on chai ("How hard could it be?"). Founder-story Reels perform well.
- **Hard rules:**
  - Never fake urgency or scarcity: no invented "37/100 claimed" counters. Show real caps, real deadlines, real counts.
  - Never call real "Booking Soon" cafés fake or test listings.
