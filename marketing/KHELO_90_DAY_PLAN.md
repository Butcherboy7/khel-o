## 2. Strategy in one paragraph

*Marketing as investing, not campaigns.* Prove it in **one neighbourhood cluster** before touching the rest of Hyderabad. Use your face and your voice (founder content) to bring local gamers to vote for and book **specific named cafés**. Turn those votes and bookings into proof for café owners. Use that proof to pitch the next cafés, then chains. Each step funds the credibility for the next.

**Why one cluster:** you can't afford to be thin everywhere (₹500/week, 2–4 h/day). Owners and players trust "people near me are doing this."

**Recommended pilot: the Kompally belt (pincode 500100).** DG (live) shares it with two lead listings (Wlid Gaming, BOUNCE Bowling, 3 votes each). *Hypothesis to confirm:* it has colleges and young people, so you can fill college-hour empty seats. If you know a better cluster, swap it, and the plan still holds.

**Market-quality check (problem size × frequency):** booking one ₹150 session is a small, occasional problem for a player, so the player-side reason to return is weak (as you said). The stronger pain is the owner's: empty seats on college/office hours, every day. So **sell the owner "fill my empty hours with no risk," and sell the player "a good session, a good deal, with my squad,"** and treat events and community as the return reason.

## 3. The plan by phase

### Weeks 1–2 (Oct 8–21): Unblock + identity sprint
| Move | Mechanism | Number to watch |
|---|---|---|
| **Stop quoting raw visitors.** Report mobile sessions, café-opened rate, votes, paid bookings only. | Bots hide the truth | Weekly scoreboard exists |
| **Check Meta targeting** (Hyderabad/pilot radius only) before any more spend. | Wasted spend on people who can't book | Ad location set |

| **Trust basics** on the site and profile: your name and face ("built by Uzair and Bindu, Hyderabad"), real café photos, link to each café's own Instagram, a human WhatsApp contact. | Your own belief: "no trust factor" | Café-open rate on mobile |
| **Post 5–7 founder Reels.** | Fastest way to learn what lands | Watch-through, shares, saves, follows per Reel |
| **Re-call Hyderabad cafés** (10 a week): start with the 8 leads that have votes. | Supply pipeline | Calls made, meetings booked |

### Weeks 3–6 (Oct 22–Nov 18): Pilot in one cluster
- **3–4 Reels a week**, each ending with a vote/book link for one named café (use `/c/<slug>` short links so each Reel is measured).
- **Visit DG and the two leads in person with your phone**: film real sessions (with permission). This is your content and your trust.
- **Make the "vote board" public**: "BOUNCE Bowling: 12 votes, 38 to go." Real counts only. Update weekly.
- **₹500/week rule:** spend only on a Reel that already did well organically, targeted to the pilot radius. Measure ₹ per vote and ₹ per booking. **Kill criterion: after 3 weeks, if ₹ per vote is above ₹X you set, stop paid and keep organic.** (I won't guess X; we'll set it from week 3 data.)
- **Owner pitch sheet** (one page per café): votes, who they are (play times, area), what KHEL-O fills (their empty college-hour seats), zero cost to list.

### Weeks 7–10 (Nov 19–Dec 16): Prove booking + a reason to return
- Run **one small event at DG**, e.g. a FIFA or WWE 1v1 knockout, free entry, fixed prize from the café or a sponsor (consistent with your no-pooled-money rule). It's content, a return reason, and a community seed.
- Use the cluster's numbers to pitch **2–3 more cafés** in the next cluster.
- First look at **retention**: of players who booked, how many booked again within 14 days?

### Weeks 11–13 (Dec 17–Jan 6): Decide
- Review against the scoreboard. Choose one: (a) repeat the cluster play in a second area, (b) approach a chain with real numbers, (c) fix the product step that's leaking.
- Write the **investor/mentor story from facts**: cluster results, cost per booking, repeat rate.
