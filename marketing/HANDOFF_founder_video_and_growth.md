## Founder video #1 (account: @khelo.journey)
Not about KHEL-O or gaming. Purpose: trust, retention, shares. Audience: people stuck / afraid to start. Close: "Send this to someone who's about to quit."
Voiceover, ~25 s, English with a little Hinglish he actually uses:
1. Nobody is watching you.
2. I spent years scared of what people would think of me.
3. Then I got to college and noticed nobody cared. It was all in my head.
4. So I started talking to people, even at hackathons, and the right ones showed up.
5. Now I'm building something of my own, and some days everything breaks at once and I want to quit.
6. But the day you want to quit is the day most people do.
7. Don't be average. You only live once, and you'll only be this young once.
8. (to camera) Send this to someone who's about to quit. Loop to first frame.
Decisions: no naming Razorpay or the CA; feelings not KHEL-O numbers; face only in last 2-3 s; no college footage (film fresh); hackathon footage unknown; founder is fine showing his face anywhere.
Caption hook: "nobody is watching you as closely as you think. send this to someone who's about to quit." Tags: #buildinpublic #startupjourney #youngfounder #hyderabad (sizes unchecked).
Delivery guidance: quiet, slow, talk to one friend, stress only "nobody", "quit", "average", drop pitch at line ends, keep one small imperfection, last line soft. Sound: soft lyric-free music 80-95 BPM ducked 12-15 dB, shutter click on 2-3 digicam shots, low ambient room sound, half a second of near-silence before the CTA, no loud whooshes or meme sounds. B-roll mapped line by line (corridors, hand over Post button, canteen/bus stop, hackathon badge, laptop at night, head on desk, close-up face, walking away at golden hour, face to camera).
